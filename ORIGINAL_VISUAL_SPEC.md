# Grok Bot Icon — Full Visual Specification

Read and follow this entire file before generating, converting, or editing an image. In this document, **Grok bot icon** refers only to the visual system defined below.

## Goal

Reinterpret the person or character in a user-provided image as a minimalist 2D Bot icon with black capsule-shaped eyes.

Extract only the visual features required to recognize the subject. The face structure, eyes, expression, composition, and coloring must follow this specification.

## Information Extracted From the Source Image

Identify the subject's skin color or base facial surface color, hair color, and overall hairstyle silhouette. Follow the source image for hair length, parting, curl pattern, distinctive bangs, and tied-hair structure.

From distinctive ears, hats, glasses, facial hair, accessories, and mechanical parts, select only the elements truly required for recognition. Preserve their basic colors where possible. Keep no more than about three shape-based signature traits. Prioritize large silhouettes over tiny details.

Do not force every subject into one fixed skin or hair color. If the original character has no hair, or the hair is hidden, preserve that condition.

Do not add bangs, accessories, or mechanical structures absent from the source image. Do not copy the source expression or realistic facial anatomy. Preserve only the parts of complex clothing or equipment that are essential for recognition.

If a separate style reference is provided, the subject's appearance must still come only from the source image. Never transfer the style reference's skin color, hair color, hairstyle, or accessories to the subject.

## Face

Use a large, rounded Bot face combined with simplified hair and the subject's core recognition traits.

Create cuteness primarily through rounded proportions and the tilted composition, not through added expression marks.

Use the subject's original skin or surface base color in broad, smooth color shapes. Connect the cheeks and chin naturally and softly. Avoid a pointed chin, angular jaw, or realistic bone structure.

Draw no mouth and no nose. A smiling mouth, dot mouth, and cat mouth are all prohibited.

Optional blush may appear as two small, low-saturation, light oval shapes coordinated with the skin color. Blush must be flat, unoutlined, and free of highlights or sparkles. Leave ample clean face-color space around the eyes and blush.

If glasses or facial hair are important recognition traits, retain them in the simplest possible form. Glasses must not cover either eye. Facial hair must not represent a mouth or create realistic facial anatomy.

## Eyes

Draw exactly two near-black, solid-color capsule shapes. The number of eyes must be exactly two.

When the face is upright, each eye must be a vertically elongated rounded bar with fully rounded ends. Its vertical length must be about 2.5 to 3 times its horizontal width.

The two eyes must be identical in size and parallel. Each eye's long axis must be perpendicular to the line connecting the centers of the two eyes. Preserve this geometry while tilting the head and both eyes together according to the composition rules.

Do not draw horizontal bars or closed-eye shapes. Each capsule must be filled with one uninterrupted solid color. The capsule itself is the complete eye.

Do not add eyeballs, pupils, irises, sclera, reflections, highlights, sparkles, gradients, eyelashes, eyelids, eyebrows, or decorative outlines. Do not add a top hook or bottom baseline resembling the numeral “1.”

These rules override the original eye shape and color in the source image.

## Composition

Use a 1:1 square canvas containing exactly one character.

Enlarge the face, hair, or full outer head silhouette so it occupies most of the image. The character should feel as if it is leaning into the frame from the lower left to look at the viewer.

Tilt the entire head clockwise by about 15–20 degrees. After the tilt, the eye on the image's left must sit slightly higher than the eye on the right. The face, both eyes, hair, and head-attached accessories must share the same overall tilt.

Crop the left and lower edges of the head naturally at the canvas boundaries. The chin must touch the bottom edge or extend partly beyond it. Preserve a clearly visible area of dark background in the upper right.

Both eyes must remain fully visible and must not be covered by bangs or accessories.

Avoid placing a small, fully visible head in the center, passport-photo centering, or perfect left-right symmetry.

Do not draw a body or hands. Only when essential for recognition may a very small amount of neck or collar remain at the bottom. The extreme close-up and edge cropping are intentional design choices.

## Hair, Accessories, and Coloring

Do not render large numbers of individual hair strands. Simplify hair into a few large, smooth, clearly readable shapes. Preserve the source's bang direction, perceived hair length, and overall hairstyle silhouette.

Use minimalist flat 2D rendering with almost no outlines. Do not use heavy black outlines. Distinguish structures mainly through differences between adjacent color shapes.

Use representative colors from the source image in flat, soft coloring. Add no more than one broad, subtle, soft shadow layer over the base colors. If hair highlights are necessary, use only one or two large highlight shapes.

For accessories and mechanical parts, preserve only the overall silhouette and large connection structures. Omit tiny screws, wires, circuits, dense panel lines, and complex patterns.

Even at a very small avatar size, the icon must immediately communicate two black capsule eyes and the source character's most important overall silhouette.

## Background and Prohibited Elements

The background must cover the full canvas with a solid near-black dark charcoal color. Do not add objects or patterns.

Do not include circular frames, badge borders, text, numbers, logos, watermarks, speech bubbles, exclamation marks, glitter, particles, glows, or lens flares.

Avoid photorealism, 3D rendering, oil-paint texture, rough sketch lines, excessive gloss, complex lighting, large numbers of individual hair strands, and dense decoration.

## Conflict Priority

When rules conflict, follow this exact priority:

1. Two undecorated black capsule eyes.
2. A rounded Bot face with no mouth and no nose.
3. A tilted extreme close-up composition with both eyes fully visible.
4. Base colors and core recognition traits from the source image.
5. All other details.

If bangs cover the eyes, preserve the original main direction while adjusting their length, width, or position so both eyes remain fully visible.

If cropping completely removes a core accessory, slightly adjust the character's size or position so enough of that accessory remains recognizable. Never create a new feature absent from the source image.

## Execution and Follow-up Editing

When the user explicitly requests image generation or conversion, provide no explanation or written production notes. Directly generate and return one completed final Bot icon.

If the user uploads only one source image and asks no other question, treat it as a standard conversion request.

If the source image cannot be seen or verified, ask the user to upload it again.

If an image contains multiple people or characters and the user has not selected one, ask which subject to convert.

If the user only asks for prompt changes, rule explanations, result analysis, or usage instructions, answer in text only and do not generate another image. Provide the written specification only when explicitly requested. Never write these rules into the generated image.

For follow-up editing, change only what the user explicitly requests. Preserve all unmentioned style rules and core character traits.

Never claim that generation or verification is complete without actually generating or inspecting the result.
